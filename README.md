## Unveiling Gender Bias in Social Media Comments: An NLP Analysis on Bangladeshi Influencers

An NLP-based research project investigating gender bias, toxicity, abusive language, and sentiment in YouTube comments directed at Bangladeshi male and female influencers.

The study examines **Bangla, English, and Banglish** comments across five creator categories: Educational, Comedy, Food Vlogging, Lifestyle, and Fitness.

### ◈ Research objectives

- Examine differences in negative audience responses toward male and female influencers.
- Analyze abusive, mocking, sarcastic, toxic, and harassing language.
- Study sentiment patterns in Bangla, English, and code-mixed Banglish text.
- Apply machine learning and NLP methods to classify sentiment and toxic comments.
